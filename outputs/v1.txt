# Hexagram 42 — 益 (Increase) → Hexagram 63 — 既濟 (After Completion)

## The Question: Longevity

---

## The Hexagram

Increase describes a state where benefit and gain are actively flowing. The movement is favorable — there is advantage in every undertaking, even bold ones like "crossing the great stream." Wind above, thunder below: a dynamic combination of arousal and penetration, movement given direction. This is a moment where conditions support growth, where something is being added to your situation. The energy is generous and expansive.

In the context of longevity, this hexagram speaks to a time when the conditions for sustaining and enriching life are present. There is real increase available.

---

## The Image

Wind and thunder together form Increase. The instruction is precise and practical: **when you see what is good, move toward it; when you see your errors, turn away from them.** This is not abstract self-improvement — it is the active mechanism of increase itself. Longevity is served by this discipline: imitate what works, eliminate what harms. The Image suggests that increase in life-force is not passive; it requires ongoing responsiveness and self-correction.

---

## Moving Lines

### Line 3 (Six in the third place)

*One is enriched through unfortunate events. No blame — if one is sincere and follows the middle way.*

This line tells you that increase comes through difficulty, even through what seems harmful or "evil." Setbacks, illness, misfortune — these become the means by which you are led toward what is actually good. The condition is sincerity and adherence to the path of the Mean: no extremes, no self-deception. The image of an officer presenting his credentials suggests that when you walk through difficulty with integrity, you earn a kind of recognition or standing — your character becomes credible. For longevity, this says plainly: hardship is not the enemy of a long life. Handled with honesty and balance, it becomes a teacher that strengthens you. Do not avoid difficulty — metabolize it.

### Line 6 (Nine in the top place)

*No one contributes to his increase. Indeed, someone strikes him. His heart is not kept constantly steady. Misfortune.*

This is a stark warning. At the extreme of the hexagram, the one who receives no increase — and is even attacked — is the one whose inner state is unstable and inconsistent. The heart is not kept steady. This is the collapse of the conditions that made increase possible. For longevity, this line points to a specific danger: **inconstancy of heart and purpose.** If you do not maintain inner equilibrium, no amount of external support will sustain you. Others will withdraw their aid; circumstances will turn hostile. The misfortune here is self-generated — it comes from failing to regulate what is within.

---

## The Resulting Hexagram — 63: 既濟 (After Completion)

After Completion describes a moment where everything has reached its proper place — fire below water, each element in correct relation. Success is present, but specifically in small matters. Perseverance is needed. The critical teaching: **at the beginning, good fortune; at the end, disorder.**

The Image instructs the superior person to think of misfortune in advance and guard against it. This is not pessimism — it is the awareness that completion carries within it the seed of decline.

For longevity, this resulting hexagram is deeply relevant. It says: you may reach a state of fulfillment, of things being in order. But that very completion is fragile. The danger is not in failing to achieve — it is in what happens after achieving. Disorder creeps in at the end if vigilance is abandoned. The counsel is to attend to small things, stay correct, and never assume the work is done.

---

## Synthesis

The reading moves from Increase — a time of active, available benefit — into After Completion, where things have come into order but face the threat of gradual decay. The two moving lines trace the arc: Line 3 says your difficulties are not obstacles to longevity but instruments of it, provided you stay sincere and balanced. Line 6 warns that inconstancy of heart undoes everything — it is the specific mechanism by which increase turns to loss. The resulting hexagram confirms that even when you have achieved balance and fullness, the real work becomes maintenance and foresight. Longevity is not won once; it is sustained through unceasing, quiet attention.

---

## Counsel

- **Embrace hardship as medicine.** What is difficult refines you — do not flee from it, but meet it with sincerity and moderation. This is how real vitality is built.
- **Stabilize your heart.** The single greatest threat to your longevity is inner inconstancy — wavering commitments, emotional turbulence, lack of steady purpose. This must be addressed directly.
- **Do not rest on achievement.** When things feel complete and ordered, that is precisely when you must think ahead, guard against complacency, and tend to small matters.
- **Imitate what is good. Discard what harms you.** This is not eventual advice — it is the daily practice that sustains increase over time.
